# House rules — `launch-timeline-and-governance`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/launch-timeline-and-governance/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Use our gate names and lead times, not the illustrative windows in the skill.
- A gate is never reported by averaging workstream colours. Any launch-blocking
  red makes the gate red.
- Gate criteria are approved by the launch committee at least four weeks
  before the gate meets.
- MLR planning assumes our actual median review cycle, not the target cycle.
- Every RACI line names a person, and every escalation route names a meeting
  that occurs before the decision deadline.
