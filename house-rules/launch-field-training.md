# House rules — `launch-field-training`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/launch-field-training/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

## Seeded examples

- Launch certification requires a scored scenario assessment by a qualified
  medical reviewer. Module completion is reported, never counted as certification.
- Every unanswerable six-month question has a rehearsed "we don't have that
  data" answer that names what is known.
- The unsolicited off-label scenario and the adverse event scenario are
  mandatory for every MSL, every launch, including experienced staff.
- Training claims may not be stronger than the approved scientific platform.
