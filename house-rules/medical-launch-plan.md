# House rules — `medical-launch-plan`

Rules here override the defaults in `skills/medical-launch-plan/SKILL.md`.
See [README.md](README.md) for how to write a good one.

This is the place to record which launch workstreams Medical Affairs owns,
which it supports, the names of your launch gates, and who the human final
judge is at each one.

## Seeded examples

- The Launch Medical Director is the final judge at every gate. Workers and the
  Launch Lead draft; they never approve, certify or send.
- Every gap in the plan names a person and a date. "Medical", "the team" or an
  agent name is not an owner.
- The indication wording is a planning assumption until the regulator confirms
  it. Every output that depends on it says so on its first page.
- The Readiness Auditor's verdict is reported to the human unedited, including
  "not ready".
- Payer value and HEOR are owned by Market Access here; the Value & Access worker
  produces medical input to their plan, not the plan itself.
