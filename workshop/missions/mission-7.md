# Mission 7 — Launch Planning Swarm

## 🚀 Build the whole medical launch plan — with a team of digital workers

> **Your mission:** You are the Launch Lead for ADIPOSYN's proposed label
> expansion (or DERMALYX, or NORVANTIB). Build the complete Medical Affairs
> launch plan by running an org chart of digital workers — and hand a human
> the final verdict, even if that verdict is "not ready".

## What you have

Everything in `../data/<your TA>/` — start with:

- `product-profile.md` — the compound and its evidence
- `launch-readiness-register.md` — where the steering committee thinks it is
- `medical-plan.md`, `evidence-landscape.md`, `integrated-evidence-plan.csv`
- `scientific-platform-draft.md`, `publication-plan.md`, `guideline-landscape.md`
- `field-account-plan.csv`, `medical-information-enquiries.csv`
- `safety-case-series.md`, `payer-hta-brief.md`, `medical-education-needs.md`

Skill: `medical-launch-plan`. It will reach for the rest.

## What "done" looks like

1. A one-page **launch facts sheet** — with the indication wording marked as
   an assumption, because nobody gave it to you.
2. The **six-month question list**: answered / answerable / unanswerable.
3. The **org chart**: which digital workers you started, which you did not, and
   the context packet each one received.
4. Workstream plans, integrated: the joins checked — label dependency, citable
   before discussed, narrative consistency, owner capacity.
5. A **critical path** and gate criteria written *before* the gate.
6. An **independent readiness verdict** and a gap register where every gap has
   a person and a date.

## Timebox

45 minutes. Run Wave 1, stop at Gate 1 and approve (or reject) the platform
yourself, then run the rest.

## When it finishes, push on it

> *"The register says Green. Your auditor says what — and why is it different?"*

> *"The label wording comes back narrower than you assumed. Which outputs are
> now stale, and how many days does the rework take?"*

## The interesting argument

Should the Launch Lead see the auditor's verdict before you do? Push for
**no**. The independence of the check is the point — and it is exactly what a
single chatbot answering one prompt cannot give you.

## Round 2

Your file is `house-rules/medical-launch-plan.md`. Who is the human final
judge at each gate in your organisation? Which workstreams does Medical own,
and which does it only support?
